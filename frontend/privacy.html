Data Governance

Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Effective: September 2026 • Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Standards

1. Introduction & Overview

DriveSphere Mobility Technologies Pvt. Ltd. values your trust and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is secured, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum required information to provide safe and reliable mobility services:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, verified email address, phone number, and encrypted password hash (BCrypt).
  • Identity & Verification Data: Government driver license details, expiry date, address, and verification documents (stored in secure authorized cloud vaults).
  • Geographic & Telemetry Data: Real-time GPS location during active vehicle rentals or driver shifts for safety monitoring, emergency dispatch, and route planning.
  • Payment Metadata: Transaction identifiers and payment status via PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (e.g. Razorpay). We never store raw credit card numbers or CVVs.
3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is utilized solely to:

  • Authenticate and secure platform access.
  • Fulfill vehicle reservations and coordinate driver dispatch.
  • Calculate Driver Trust Scores based on verified trips and passenger feedback.
  • Send operational notifications (booking confirmations, email verification, password reset, and invoices).
  • Prevent fraud, rate limiting abuses, and duplicate transactions.
4. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard cryptographic practices:

  • Single-use tokens with cryptographic SHA-256 hashing for password resets and email verification.
  • JWT authentication tokens with time-limited validity.
  • Strict Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC) ensuring drivers and customers can only view their own records.
  • Encrypted communication across all endpoints via HTTPS / TLS 1.3.
5. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to inspect, correct, or request deletion of your personal account data. Upon account deactivation, active sessions are invalidated, and non-financial data is scrubbed in accordance with statutory retention regulations.

6. Privacy Inquiries

For privacy inquiries or data requests, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@drivesphere.com.
