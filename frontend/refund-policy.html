Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Production Rules & Gateway Reconciliation

At DriveSphere, we understand that travel plans can change unexpectedly. Our automated cancellation and refund policy is tiered based on notice period prior to the scheduled reservation start time to balance fairness for customers and compensation for reserved fleet inventory and driver partners.

Tiered Refund Schedule
Notice Period Refund Eligibility Policy Details
More than 24 hours 100% Full Refund Full refund of vehicle rental, security deposit, and driver accompaniment fees. No cancellation deduction.
2 to 24 hours prior 80% Partial Refund 80% refund credited. A 20% platform holding fee applies to cover reserved fleet allocation.
Under 2 hours prior No Refund (0%) Due to last-minute dispatch lock and driver route assignment, no refund is granted for cancellations under 2 hours.
Automated Refund Processing

When a paid booking is cancelled through the Customer Workspace:

  • The backend immediately recalculates the eligible refund percentage based on exact server timestamp delta.
  • The payment record is transitioned to REFUNDED or PARTIALLY_REFUNDED state.
  • If a live Razorpay transaction exists, an automated refund request is submitted through the Razorpay Payment Gateway API with cryptographic verification.
  • Refund credits reflect in the original funding account within 5 to 7 business days depending on the customer's bank.
Fleet and Driver State Integrity

Upon booking cancellation, the allocated vehicle is immediately returned to AVAILABLE status. If a driver partner was assigned, their assignment is released; however, their status is set to AVAILABLE only if their driver session is currently active and online.

Support & Discrepancies

If you experience any delay or discrepancy with your refund reconciliation, reach out with your Booking ID to refunds@drivesphere.com.
